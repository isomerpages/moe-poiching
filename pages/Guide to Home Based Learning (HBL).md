<h3><strong>5. Important SLS (Student Learning Space) Information</strong></h3>
<p>For an SLS assignment to be recorded as <strong>complete</strong>, students
must ensure that:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>You click <strong>“Mark Activity as Complete".</strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You interact with and complete <strong>every activity</strong> (e.g. watch
all videos).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Skipping activities will result in the assignment <strong>not being recorded.</strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>An assignment is only considered completed when the status shows <strong>“COMPLETE”.</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
